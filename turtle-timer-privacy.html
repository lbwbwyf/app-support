TURTLE RACE TIMER / PRIVACY

Privacy, in plain words.

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Effective September 17, 2026 · Applies to iOS / iPadOS app version 1.0

01. About this policy

This policy applies to Turtle Race Timer for iPhone and iPad, developed by Yifan Wang. It also explains what happens when you contact support or visit this support website.

02. Data in the app

The app does not require an account. It does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer, and it contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location.

03. What stays on your device

The app stores your language, sound, scene, and route preferences, plus recently used timer durations, locally on your device. These settings are used to personalize the app and make it easier to reuse a duration. There is no developer-operated cloud storage or synchronization. Device backups managed by Apple may include this data, depending on your settings.

04. Your choices and deletion

You can change preferences in the app. Deleting the app (rather than offloading it) removes its local app data from the device. Copies in device backups are managed through your Apple backup settings. The developer cannot access or delete data stored only on your device.

05. Purchases and Apple services

The app is a paid download without subscriptions or in-app purchases. Apple processes App Store payments; the developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple may process store, diagnostic, or backup information according to your choices and its own policies. See Apple’s privacy policy.

06. When you contact support

If you email 2423678715@qq.com, the developer receives your email address and any message or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and troubleshoot issues, not for advertising. Email is handled by the QQ Mail service. Support messages are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the same address.

07. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not use analytics scripts, advertising cookies, or contact forms. GitHub may log technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, for security and operating the hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link or sending an email is subject to the relevant service’s policies.

08. Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has shared personal information in a support message, contact the developer to request its deletion.

09. Policy updates

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.
