TURTLE RACE TIMER / SUPPORT

A little help, right here.

Getting started and common questions about Turtle Race Timer.

How do I start a race?

Choose a duration from 1 minute to 24 hours, then tap “Start race”. Work on your task while the turtle moves toward the finish. When you finish, tap “Done!” before time runs out to send the rabbit racing to the line.

How do I change the look or language?

Open Settings to change the language, scene, route, or sound. Choose Meadow, Snow, or Autumn and a Hairpins, Flow, or Loop route. The app supports 14 languages. When following the system, unsupported languages fall back to English.

Why can’t I hear the sound effects?

Check that Sound is enabled in the app, turn up your device volume, and check Silent mode and any connected headphones or Bluetooth speakers.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account is required. The timer and its scenes work on your device without an internet connection. Downloading the app or its updates from the App Store requires a connection.

Where are my settings saved?

Your language, scene, route, sound preference, and recently used durations are saved on your device. There is no developer-operated account or cloud sync. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your settings.

What if I switch apps or lock my screen?

For the intended animated race experience, keep the app open and the screen awake. Do not rely on it as a background alarm or notification service.

How do purchases and refunds work?

Turtle Race Timer is a paid download with no subscription or in-app purchases. Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. For purchase help, visit Apple’s billing support.

How can I report a problem?

Email 2423678715@qq.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. A screenshot can help; remove any private information before sending it.
